During the 2026 Bundibugyo virus disease (BDBV) outbreak, the healthsites.io API serves
OpenStreetMap health facility data to the
[INRB BDBV2026 epidemic dashboard](https://inrb-umie.github.io/BDBV2026-Epidemic_Dashboard/).
The data is mapped and maintained by the OpenStreetMap community; healthsites.io runs the
infrastructure that delivers it. It sits alongside GRID3 COD Health Facilities as a
complementary layer. The dashboard work is published in
[The Lancet Infectious Diseases](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/laninf/article/PIIS1473-3099(26)00330-0/fulltext).

## What we are doing in DRC

### DRC Health Facility Data Pipeline — live

A repeatable pipeline that exports OpenStreetMap health facility data from the healthsites.io
API and prepares it for the dashboard's analysis across the country's health zones.
[How the pipeline works →](https://github.com/healthsites/drc-ebola-2026/wiki/DRC-Health-Facility-Data-Pipeline)

### Campaign support — live

<!-- TODO (Mark): one or two sentences on what campaign support covers today. -->
Support for OpenStreetMap health facility mapping in DRC, so that the data behind the
response stays current.

### Open identifier infrastructure — in development

Stable, open identifiers that link the same facility across OpenStreetMap, GRID3 and national
registries, so datasets can be joined without manual matching.
