### The user story

<blockquote class="campaign-quote">As a pregnant mother I want to know where the nearest emergency health service is, so that I can plan for potential complications during childbirth.</blockquote>

In 2022, 12 OpenStreetMap community members validated 398 health facilities across Saint-Louis. Each of these 35 met four tests: a clinic, doctors' practice or hospital, with at least one bed, emergency services and a published phone number. Nobody has yet confirmed with the health system that they can manage a pregnancy emergency.

### Why heat matters

High temperatures in pregnancy raise the risk of preterm birth and stillbirth. A review of 70 studies found that the odds of a preterm birth rose by about 5% for each 1 °C rise in temperature, and by 16% during heatwaves ([Chersich et al., BMJ 2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.m3811)).

The map and the figures above show how hot each facility was over one recent week, compared with every mapped health facility in Senegal. One week in November is not the hot season, which runs from April to June.

### What Étape 2 does

- **Confirms the 35** with Saint-Louis's health system: which can manage a pregnancy emergency today.
- **Updates OpenStreetMap**, so the confirmed list is open data under the Open Database Licence, served by the healthsites.io API.
- **Puts the evidence to work** in the NSO–Ministry of Health–OpenStreetMap interoperability work described on the [Saint-Louis page](/campaigns/senegal/saint-louis/).

### What is on this page

Names, types and locations come from OpenStreetMap, served by the healthsites.io API. The 2022 survey also recorded phone numbers, beds and staff. They are not published here, because Étape 2 confirms them first.
