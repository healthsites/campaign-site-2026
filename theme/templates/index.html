A Digital Public Good for Health Data Infrastructure

Healthsites.io maintains an open, community-validated registry of 1.18 million health facilities across 204 countries. Our open data adheres to privacy and best practices, does no harm by design, and enables interoperability with national health information systems. Listed in the digital public goods registry.
