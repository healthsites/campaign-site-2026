    How to read this map

    • Colours are relative. They compare each facility with the others in Senegal. A facility in the coolest group can still be dangerously hot.
    • Values are corrected for local conditions. HeatReady downscales the 9 km ERA5-Land grid, which reads below the daytime highs measured at Senegal's weather stations, most of all inland. Where the correction has not been tested, the map uses the grid value and the popup says so. Facilities in the same 9 km square share one value.
    • Nights and heat stress matter as much as the daytime high. Warm nights give no relief, and WBGT takes humidity, sun and wind into account. In dry months like November, the heat index reads below the air temperature, so it is in the download but not on the map.
    • The top 10 depends on where facilities are mapped. Saint-Louis has more mapped facilities than any region outside Dakar, because they were checked in the field in 2022, so it has more chances to appear in the top 10. Use the region table to compare regions. Where facilities are missing from OpenStreetMap, the heat picture is missing too.

    Saint-Louis Étape 2: evidence and policy engagement

    In 2022, 35 facilities in Saint-Louis were identified as likely to support emergency pregnancy referrals, and many of them face some of the highest heat in Senegal. Étape 2 confirms with the health system which of them can, and gives the Ministry of Health the evidence to make its case to the Ministry of Finance, with support from the National Statistics Office.
