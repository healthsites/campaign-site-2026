    How to read this map

    • Colours are relative. They compare each facility with the others in Senegal. A facility in the coolest group can still be dangerously hot.
    • Nearby facilities share a value. Temperatures come from a weather grid of about 9 km, so facilities in the same grid square have the same temperature.
    • Some facilities are missing. The map shows the facilities in OpenStreetMap. Tambacounda, Kolda and Kédougou have not yet had a validation campaign, so their coverage is likely to be less complete.

    Validate the facilities that face the most heat

    Data stewards and community health workers check every facility in a region, paid for their work. In Tambacounda, Kolda and Kédougou they would start with the facilities facing the most heat.
